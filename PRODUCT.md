# Product

## Register

product

## Platform

web

## Users

Everyday people in Spain buying and selling used goods — phones, furniture, bikes, clothes, games. The same person is often both buyer and seller, and they arrive from a phone as much as a desktop. Buyers want to find something nearby, judge whether the seller and item are trustworthy, and pay without fear of being scammed. Sellers want to list an item in about a minute and get paid quickly. The interface is in Spanish. Success is a completed, confident transaction between two strangers: a listing published, a message answered, an offer accepted, a payment cleared.

## Product Purpose

espublicar is a C2C second-hand marketplace (in the OLX / Wallapop family) where users list products, browse and buy nearby, negotiate prices through offers, message sellers, and manage orders from a dashboard. It exists to make peer-to-peer resale feel safe and effortless: secure payment with Bizum or card, tracked shipping, and price negotiation built in. Success looks like both sides trusting the flow enough to complete the deal on-platform rather than taking it elsewhere.

## Positioning

The second-hand marketplace where paying a stranger feels as safe as paying a shop — trust and secure payment are built into every step, not bolted on.

## Brand Personality

Trustworthy and friendly. Warm, local, and approachable, never slick or corporate. The voice reassures without patronizing; it makes a nervous first-time buyer feel safe and a busy seller feel it will be quick. Confidence comes from clarity and visible proof (secure payment, tracked shipping, ratings), not from hype.

## Anti-references

Do not look like any of these. Not a **generic SaaS template** — no blue-gradient hero, identical icon-card grids, or landing-page-by-numbers scaffolding. Not a **luxury or premium boutique** — high-end fashion minimalism reads wrong for used goods and alienates ordinary sellers. Not a **cold corporate or bank** interface — stiff, grey, impersonal enterprise UI kills the neighborly feel. Not **cheap or spammy classifieds** — the ad-dense, cluttered, low-trust OLX/Craigslist look is exactly what secure payment and clean listings are meant to escape.

## Design Principles

Show the trust, don't claim it — surface secure-payment, tracked-shipping, ratings, and verification as concrete UI, not marketing copy. Keep listing and buying nearly frictionless — a sale should take about a minute; every extra field or step is a leak. Use familiar marketplace patterns — people come from OLX/Wallapop; meet their muscle memory instead of reinventing navigation. Design mobile-first — most traffic is a phone in a hand, so touch targets, scannable cards, and thumb-reach come first. Be honest over impressive — plain labels, real photos, clear prices; polish serves comprehension, never decoration.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.1 AA: body text ≥4.5:1 contrast, large text ≥3:1, visible keyboard focus on all interactive elements, and labelled form controls. Honor `prefers-reduced-motion` — every animation (hero float, blob pulse, reveals) needs a reduced/instant alternative. Spanish-language UI; keep copy plain enough for non-technical users.
